# Information Security Risk Register

**Project Type:** Simulated GRC Assessment  
**Assessment Scope:** IT Systems and Business Applications  
**Assessment Purpose:** Identify, assess, and document information security risks.

## 1. Risk Assessment Methodology

Risks are evaluated using two factors:

- Likelihood: Probability of the risk occurring.
- Impact: Potential business consequences if the risk occurs.

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

### Risk Rating

| Score | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

## 2. Risk Register

| Risk ID | Identified Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Rating |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| R-01 | Unauthorized access to business applications | 4 | 5 | 20 | Critical |
| R-02 | Delayed removal of access for terminated employees | 3 | 4 | 12 | High |
| R-03 | Inadequate backup and recovery procedures | 3 | 5 | 15 | High |
| R-04 | Lack of periodic user access reviews | 4 | 4 | 16 | High |
| R-05 | Insufficient security awareness among employees | 3 | 3 | 9 | Medium |

## 3. Risk Treatment and Mitigation

| Risk ID | Recommended Mitigation |
|---|---|
| R-01 | Implement role-based access control and multi-factor authentication. |
| R-02 | Establish a formal employee offboarding and access revocation process. |
| R-03 | Implement regular backups and periodically test restoration procedures. |
| R-04 | Conduct periodic user access reviews and retain approval evidence. |
| R-05 | Conduct regular information security awareness training. |

## 4. Risk Monitoring

The organization should:

1. Review the risk register periodically.
2. Assign risk owners for identified risks.
3. Monitor the implementation of mitigation actions.
4. Update risk ratings when circumstances change.
5. Retain evidence of risk treatment and management approval.

## 5. Conclusion

The assessment identified five illustrative information security risks across IT systems and business applications.

Unauthorized access was rated as Critical, while other risks ranged from Medium to High.

Management should prioritize remediation based on risk severity and monitor the effectiveness of implemented controls.
